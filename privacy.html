Privacy

Plain English, no fine print.

Ledger Deck doesn’t collect anything. There are no accounts, no servers holding your data, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you add (statements, balances, categories, tax figures, your AI key) is stored in your browser’s storage on your device. If you set a passcode, it’s encrypted there with AES-256. It never reaches the people who make Ledger Deck.

When something leaves your device

  • Questions to the AI. When you use Ask or the tax assistant, your question goes directly from your browser to the AI company you chose, using your own key. Unless you turn it off, a summary of your figures goes with it (totals by month and category, account balances and invoices, not individual transactions). That company’s own privacy policy applies to what you send.
  • OpenAI voice. If you choose the OpenAI voice for briefings, the briefing text is sent to OpenAI to be read aloud. The device voice stays on your device.
  • Reminder emails. Drafting an invoice reminder opens your own email app. Ledger Deck doesn’t send email.

The app itself is loaded from a static web host, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) like any website. No financial data is ever part of those requests.

Backups

Backups are files you download and keep where you choose. They’re encrypted with a password you set. Without that password, no one can open them, including us.

Bank passwords

Ledger Deck never asks for your bank password and never logs in to your bank. You download statements yourself and import them.

Deleting everything

Use Delete everything on this device in Data & AI, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Not advice

Ledger Deck provides general information only. It isn’t financial, tax or legal advice, and it isn’t a registered tax agent. Talk to a registered tax agent or licensed financial adviser before acting.
